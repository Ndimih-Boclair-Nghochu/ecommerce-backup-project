# Professional Design System - MyShop

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Brand Blue**: `#1E40AF` (blue-700) - Professional, trustworthy, calming
- **Brand Blue Dark**: `#1E3A8A` (blue-800) - For hover states
- **Brand Blue Light**: `#3B82F6` (blue-500) - For accents

### Secondary Colors
- **Action Orange**: `#F97316` (orange-500) - CTAs, important actions
- **Action Orange Hover**: `#EA580C` (orange-600) - Hover state

### Neutral Colors
- **Text Dark**: `#111827` (gray-900) - Primary text
- **Text Medium**: `#374151` (gray-700) - Secondary text
- **Text Light**: `#6B7280` (gray-600) - Tertiary text
- **Background Light**: `#F3F4F6` (gray-50) - Page background
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (white) - Card backgrounds

### Accent Colors
- **Success**: `#10B981` (green-500) - Success states, in stock
- **Error**: `#EF4444` (red-500) - Errors, out of stock
- **Warning**: `#FBBF24` (amber-400) - Warnings, special offers
- **Info**: `#3B82F6` (blue-500) - Information

## Component Colors

### Header
- Background: White with shadow
- Logo: Blue gradient (blue-700 to blue-600)
- Navigation links: Gray hover to blue-700
- Cart button: Orange background with orange-600 hover

### Hero Section
- Background: Blue gradient (blue-700 → blue-600 → blue-800)
- CTA Button: Orange (orange-500) with shadow
- Secondary Button: White border with hover effect
- Badge: Orange background

### Product Cards
- Category Badge: Blue (blue-700)
- Price Text: Blue (blue-700)
- Stock Bar: Blue gradient
- Wishlist: Red (red-500) when favorited
- Quick View Button: Blue hover

### Buttons
- Primary: Blue gradient with blue-800 hover
- Secondary: Orange with orange-600 hover
- Danger: Red with red-600 hover

### Features Section
- Each feature has subtle gradient backgrounds:
  - Delivery: Blue gradient
  - Payment: Green gradient
  - Returns: Purple gradient
  - Gifts: Orange gradient

## Typography
- Headings: Bold, Gray-900 (dark)
- Body Text: Gray-700 (medium)
- Small Text: Gray-600 (light)
- Font Weight: Semibold for navigation, Bold for headings

## Spacing & Borders
- Border Radius: lg (8px) for cards, full for badges
- Border Color: Subtle gray-100 or colored borders
- Shadow: md for cards, lg for important elements
- Transitions: All smooth (300ms)

## Inspiration
This design system is inspired by:
- Amazon (Professional blue, clear hierarchy)
- Shopify (Clean, modern aesthetic)
- Alibaba (Accessible colors, trust-focused)

## Key Principles
✅ Professional & trustworthy (blue primary)
✅ High conversion (orange CTAs stand out)
✅ Clean & minimal (white space, clear hierarchy)
✅ Accessible (good contrast ratios)
✅ Modern (gradients, smooth transitions)
✅ Consistent (unified color usage)
