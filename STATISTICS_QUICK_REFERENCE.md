## ⚡ Quick Tips

**Filter Faster**:
- Select region first (speeds up results)
- Both dates required
- Use for specific time periods

**Compare Better**:
- Compare shows last year by default
- Adjust period selector before comparing
- Look for red numbers = problem areas

**Export Smarter**:
- Set up your view first
- Then download
- Reports include your filters
- Can open in browser or print
